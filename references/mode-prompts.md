# MODE-SPECIFIC PROMPT STRUCTURES

Templates for the cinematic body of the Phase 4 prompt. The labeled `@reference:` block (from SKILL.md Phase 4) always goes at the TOP. The body below is what changes per mode.

---

## `tv_spot` — Broadcast commercial (15s default)

5-beat narrative structure. NO dialogue / NO voiceover in the prompt (Marketing Studio doesn't generate audio — VO added in post).

```
Cinematic [N]-second [genre] TV spot for [Brand], [one-sentence positioning].

Open on [shot 1: establishing beat, specific camera move, lighting, action].

Cut to [shot 2: reveal or product introduction, specific camera move, environment, motion].

Cut to [shot 3: detail/macro montage, rising tempo, micro-shots of features, textures, kinetic moments].

Cut to [shot 4: hero beat, emotional or kinetic crescendo, slow-motion direction, lighting].

End on [shot 5: payoff and logo reveal with typography direction and tagline visible on the final frame as part of the brand lockup — render as permanent brand marks, not text overlays].

Style: [genre reference], [color grade], [lens characteristics], [score with instrumentation], [sound design — note this for post: real fire/engine/ambient effects], no dialogue rendered in video.
```

**Required:** 5 beats max. Specific camera move per beat. Real tagline locked into final frame as brand mark.

## `ugc` — Authentic creator content (15s default)

Sequential dialogue script with anatomy block. Spoken script will be added in post via VO, but the visual stage direction goes here.

```
Authentic UGC vertical 9:16 [archetype] [review/discovery/unboxing/demo] for [Brand].

Creator archetype: [age, gender, hair, styling, outfit, energy]. [Explicit phone-hand and product-hand assignment].

Setting: [environment, time of day, lighting, atmosphere, background, product placement].

Hook (0–3s): [Stage direction with product positioning].

Demo (3–8s): [Stage direction with product interaction — what they're showing].

Emotional payoff (8–12s): [Stage direction with facial expression — transformation, surprise, contrast].

CTA (12–15s): [Stage direction with product hero in frame].

Style: authentic iPhone-shot [archetype] UGC aesthetic — [handheld characteristics], [environmental lighting], [DOF], [color grade], [pacing], [ambient sound notation for post]. First-person selfie camera only.

Anatomy: anatomically correct human hands with exactly five fingers per hand. One [left/right] hand [doing X]. One [right/left] hand [doing Y]. Natural finger positioning. No duplicate limbs, no mirrored hands, no extra fingers. Hands consistent throughout the video.
```

**Required:** Anatomy block at end. Explicit hand assignment. No professional polish in style block. Word count for VO (added in post): ~40 spoken words across 15s = 150 wpm.

## Universal style block additions

Append to ANY mode prompt above:

```
[For all UGC-family modes:] Anatomy: anatomically correct human hands and body, no duplicate limbs, no extra fingers, natural posing.

[For all modes:] No on-screen text overlays during the spot. Final logo and tagline render as permanent brand marks on the final frame, not as text graphics added in post.

[Audio reminder:] Audio sound design notes above are for POST-PRODUCTION. The Marketing Studio Video model does not generate audio. The output will be silent video.
```

## Word count discipline (for VO added in post)

Spoken VO cadence: ~150 wpm = ~2.5 words/second.

| Duration | Max spoken words for VO |
|---|---|
| 6s | 12–15 words |
| 15s | 35–40 words |
| 20s | 50 words |
| 30s | 75 words |

Always read the VO script aloud against a stopwatch before locking the prompt.
