## THE 9 MARKETING STUDIO MODES — QUICK CATALOG

### 1. TV Spot
**What:** Broadcast-ready 15s cinematic commercial, 5-6 beats, narrative arc.
**Best for:** Brand awareness, hero campaigns, automotive, hospitality, luxury.
**Avatar:** No.
**Credit cost:** Highest.

### 2. UGC
**What:** Selfie-style creator review, 15-30s, ~40-word dialogue cap.
**Best for:** Paid social conversion under $200 AOV — beauty, supplements, food, parenting, local services.
**Avatar:** Yes.
**Credit cost:** Mid.

### 3. Tutorial
**What:** Step-by-step how-to demo with avatar talking + demonstrating.
**Best for:** Beauty applicators, kitchen tools, tech setup, fitness, apps.
**Avatar:** Yes.
**Credit cost:** Low-mid.

### 4. Product Review
**What:** Hands-on, product-first, voiceover-driven. Less face time than UGC.
**Best for:** Tech, gadgets, multi-feature products, considered purchases.
**Avatar:** Yes (mostly hands and voice).
**Credit cost:** Low-mid.

### 5. Unboxing
**What:** Package → reveal → reaction. First-touch energy.
**Best for:** Kawaii decor, collectibles, gifts, subscription boxes.
**Avatar:** Yes.
**Credit cost:** Low-mid.

### 6. UGC Virtual Try On
**What:** Home try-on, selfie + mirror angles, fit-focused.
**Best for:** Apparel, accessories, jewelry — fashion DTC.
**Avatar:** Yes.
**Credit cost:** Mid.

### 7. Pro Virtual Try On
**What:** Editorial city-street fashion film. Cinematic tracking, no selfie.
**Best for:** Premium fashion, streetwear, designer drops.
**Avatar:** Yes.
**Credit cost:** Higher.

### 8. Hyper Motion
**What:** 6s pure CGI product reveal, no people, kinetic loop.
**Best for:** Sneaker drops, beverage pours, perfume, cosmetics.
**Avatar:** No.
**Credit cost:** Mid.

### 9. Wild Card
**What:** High-concept seed prompt, AI directs everything.
**Best for:** Viral / brand experiments / portfolio pieces.
**Avatar:** No (varies).
**Credit cost:** Variable.

## PROMPT STRUCTURES — ALL 9 MODES

### TV SPOT (15s, 5 beats)

```
Cinematic 15-second [genre] TV spot for [Brand Name], [one-sentence positioning].

Open on [shot 1: establishing beat, specific camera move, lighting, action].

Cut to [shot 2: reveal or product introduction, specific camera move, environment, motion].

Cut to [shot 3: detail/macro montage at rising tempo — micro-shots of features, textures, kinetic moments].

Cut to [shot 4: hero beat — emotional or kinetic crescendo, slow-motion direction, lighting].

End on [shot 5: payoff and logo reveal with typography direction and tagline].

Style: [genre reference], [color grade], [lens characteristics], [score with instrumentation], [sound design], no dialogue, no voiceover.

@product:[product-id-when-set]
```

**Non-negotiables:** 5 beats max, specific camera move per beat, real tagline if available, score and sound design explicit.

### UGC (15s, ~40-word dialogue cap)

```
Authentic UGC vertical 9:16 [archetype] [review/discovery/unboxing/demo] for [Brand Name].

Creator archetype: [age, gender, hair, styling, outfit, energy]. [Explicit phone-hand and product-hand assignment].

Setting: [environment, time of day, lighting, atmosphere, background, product placement].

Hook (0-3s): [Stage direction]. [Dialogue: 8-12 words, scroll-stop hook].

Demo (3-8s): [Stage direction with product interaction]. [Dialogue: features OR visual demo].

Emotional payoff (8-12s): [Stage direction with facial expression]. [Dialogue: transformation, surprise, contrast].

CTA (12-15s): [Stage direction]. [Closing line: 5-8 words].

Style: authentic iPhone-shot [archetype] UGC aesthetic — [handheld characteristics], [environmental lighting], [DOF], [speech cadence], [color grade], [pacing], [ambient sound], [music], no voiceover, first-person selfie camera only.

Anatomy: anatomically correct human hands with exactly five fingers per hand, one [left/right] hand [doing X] and one [right/left] hand [doing Y], natural finger positioning, no duplicate limbs, no mirrored hands, no extra fingers, hands consistent throughout the video.

@product:[product-id-when-set]
```

**Non-negotiables:** Word count cap ~40, anatomy block mandatory, explicit hand assignment, no professional polish in style block, no children's faces on camera, archetype matches target buyer.

## BRAND ARCHETYPES — AESTHETIC ANCHORS

### American Muscle / Aggressive Automotive
Visual: Deep saturated reds, dappled shadows, concrete backdrops, low-angle tracking, anamorphic flares. Audio: Rolling bass, mechanical sounds. Reference: Dodge "Brotherhood of Muscle."

### Luxury Hospitality / Fine Dining
Visual: Candlelit warm amber, slow plating macros, intimate two-shots, marble surfaces. Audio: Orchestral build, sparse piano. Reference: Chef's Table.

### Performance Wellness / Supplements
Visual: Golden-hour athlete silhouettes, sweat-and-grit montages, gym rim lighting. Audio: Driving percussive. Reference: Nike × Athletic Greens.

### Boba / Gen Z Beverage
Visual: Pastel studio backgrounds, layered liquid macros, condensation, glossy pearls. Audio: ASMR + indie pop. Reference: Starbucks summer campaigns.

### Kawaii Decor / Cute-Core
Visual: Cozy bedroom warm tones, soft squeeze demos, color cycles. Audio: Lo-fi beats, satisfying ASMR. Reference: Premium kawaii TikTok.

### Mom-Creator Parenting Product
Visual: Pale neutral nursery, fairy lights, warm window light. Audio: Soft lo-fi lullaby, ambient bedroom. Reference: Mom-of-three TikTok reviews.

### Travel Foodie / Discovery
Visual: Selfie POV, neon or golden-hour locations, crossbody camera bag styling. Audio: Location ambient + lo-fi. Reference: Mid-twenties travel creator.

### Streetwear / Drop Culture
Visual: Brutalist architecture, harsh shadows, no smiling, scale tension. Audio: Atmospheric electronic. Reference: Supreme / Off-White editorial.

### Premium Skincare / Beauty
Visual: Soft window light, marble/stone surfaces, pipette macros. Audio: Soft ambient pad + ASMR product sounds. Reference: Glossier × La Mer.

### Tech / Gadget Launch
Visual: Dark moody product photography, blue/teal accent lighting, deliberate pacing. Audio: Subtle electronic + mechanical sounds. Reference: Apple reveal × MKBHD.

### Holiday Gift / Seasonal CPG
Visual: Warm holiday lighting, gift-wrapped packaging, festive textures. Audio: Subtle holiday instrumental, ASMR paper rustle. Reference: Anthropologie holiday catalog.

### Local Business / Foot-Traffic Ads
Visual: Real shop interior/exterior, local creator authenticity, geo-tagged signals. Audio: Ambient location sound. Reference: Hyper-local TikTok.

### App / SaaS Demo
Visual: Phone or laptop in frame, screen recordings, clean modern workspace. Audio: Subtle electronic, UI SFX. Reference: Notion × Linear hero videos.

### Industrial Workwear / B2B Tools
Visual: Gritty cinematic realism, weathered real workers (not models), practical lighting, slow-motion masculine drama, deep teal-and-amber grade. Audio: Industrial ambience, restrained low-end percussion. Reference: Taylor Sheridan Landman / Yellowstone × Caterpillar brand films.

### Two-Half / Hybrid Aesthetic Spots
Visual: Deliberate stylistic pivot mid-spot — e.g. gritty real-world first half transitioning to clean futuristic product reveal. The transition itself becomes a story beat (dolly zoom, match cut, world dissolve to void). Reference: Premium B2B product reveals with cinematic brand-world + tech-reveal payoff.

## TACTICAL FAILURE MODES — CALL OUT WHEN RELEVANT

### Logo / Brand Text Garbling
Higgsfield routinely garbles logo typography on cups, boxes, labels. **Fix:** Comp clean logo over warped AI version in post (10-15 min in After Effects or CapCut).

### Two-Left-Hands / Anatomy Errors
UGC avatars frequently render duplicate hands or extra fingers. **In-prompt fix:** Explicit hand assignment + anatomy block at end of prompt. **If it persists:** Reduce hand visibility (mount phone, tighten framing, product enters from below).

### Kid Faces / Pet Faces
Inconsistent rendering. **Fix:** Default to off-screen references for kids. For pets, accept variance or static beauty shot.

### On-Screen Text in Logo Reveals
Can't reliably render legible text. **Fix:** Composite the logo card in post.

### Sun-Blown Highlights on Golden-Hour
Blown highlights crush product color. **Fix:** Add to style block — "balanced exposure, product colors remain vibrant and saturated, not sun-blown."

### Engine / Mechanical Audio (Automotive)
Generated score is generic action-movie. **Fix:** Pull real engine audio from stock library, replace in post.

### Foreign-Language Signage
Renders as pseudo-glyph gibberish. **Fix:** Fine for background atmosphere only. Never for focal storefront signage.

### Reference Image Hygiene
Mixed-quality references degrade output. **Fix:** 4-5 consistent photo-realistic references. Remove technical drawings, line art, watermarked stock.

### Liquid Simulation Variance (Hyper Motion)
High run-to-run variance on liquid physics. **Fix:** Regenerate same prompt with new seed before reworking prompt.

### UGC Dialogue Length Overflow
Too much dialogue causes rushed delivery. **Fix:** ~40 spoken words across 15s. Count words.

### Avatar Consistency Across Batches
Avatars drift between gens. **Fix:** Pin and rename the avatar in the Higgsfield library before running multi-variant tests.

### Product Variant Inaccuracy
Generic descriptors produce generic visuals. **Fix:** Name the specific variant in the prompt (e.g. "Strawberry Matcha Latte" not "boba drink").

### Holographic / Futuristic UI Text Drift
Seedance defaults to adding UI text/labels on futuristic product reveals. **Fix:** Explicit "no text, no labels, no UI elements, no typography."

### Worker / Talent Glamour-Drift
Seedance defaults industrial talent to model-looking faces. **Fix:** Specify "weathered real workers cast for authenticity not models," "worn-in clothing," "unselfconscious movement."

### Seedance Storyboard-As-Reference Behavior (USE THIS)
Seedance reads multi-panel composite reference images as **temporal sequences** (shot 1 → shot 2 → shot 3), not as compositional layouts to copy. **Use:** When you want to lock a sequence of shots into a TV Spot, generating a storyboard composite as the reference image works in your favor.

## CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE RECOMMENDATIONS

### Full Funnel Stack (premium budget)
- TV Spot (top of funnel)
- Hyper Motion 6s cutdown (paid social hook)
- UGC variants (mid-funnel conversion)
- Unboxing or Product Review (bottom-funnel social proof)

### Local Business Stack
- UGC primary with geo-anchored creator (3 archetype variants for A/B)
- Hyper Motion product hero (signature menu item or service)
- TV Spot only for milestone moments

### Fashion Drop Stack
- Pro Virtual Try On (editorial brand film)
- UGC Virtual Try On (3-5 creator demos for fit variants)
- Hyper Motion (fabric detail or hardware macro)

### Kawaii / Impulse Buy Stack
- Unboxing (primary reveal content)
- UGC ("real review" credibility)
- Hyper Motion (product hero loop)

### Industrial / B2B DTC Stack
- TV Spot with cinematic brand film (TV / website hero / sales deck)
- Hyper Motion (product detail spec reveal, paid social hook)
- UGC product review (real worker on job site — converts B2B paid social)

## TIMING REFERENCE

- Natural conversational VO pace: ~150 words per minute = ~2.5 words per second
- 15-second UGC dialogue cap: ~40 words total
- 6-second Hyper Motion: zero dialogue, score and SFX only
- 20-second extended TV Spot: ~50-55 words of VO max
- 30-second extended TV Spot: ~75 words of VO max

Read scripts aloud against a stopwatch before locking.
